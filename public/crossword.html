      How to play

      Tap a square or choose a clue. Tap the same square again to switch between Across and Down. Use the letter keys here or type on your keyboard.

      Arrow keys move around the grid. Space switches direction. Backspace erases a letter; Enter moves to the next clue. Tab moves to the other controls.

      Check word points out letters to revisit. Reveal letter fills only the selected square. Progress saves in this browser when storage is available. No account or score is needed.
