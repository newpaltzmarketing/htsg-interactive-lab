# Release changes

## 1.5.0 — live, October 17, 2026

The pause panel allows switching Spread/Focused at any pause without losing weapon level, score or ammunition. Choosing early avoids the later automatic wave-two prompt. Missile capacity rises to eight pairs; the first weapon upgrade supplies four pairs and capsules replenish six. Supplies begin in wave two, and missiles fire more often with gentle guidance toward a living target ahead. Shields retain their distinct blue pickup.

These mechanic changes require rules **1.5.0**. Coordinated Community **1.0.5** gives new weekly attempts a versioned ID and separate board. Old history remains readable, and unexpired server-issued 1.4.0 attempts can finish without entering the new board. The coordinated installers passed source/UI acceptance and were published; the other games, approved sharing cover and public URLs are preserved.

## 1.4.1 — historical, October 17, 2026

Agency Invaders receives six agency-object threats: a paper brief creature, clock, cash-filled wallet, tangled circuit, hourglass and unplugged connector. Scope Creep becomes a paper creature; Change Request is a revision printer/stamp machine. Spread beams, focused beams and paired missiles have clearer trails, with small recoil, muzzle glow and impact effects. Falling labels are removed; Help shows the actual models and damage feedback still names the cause. Optional synthesized firing sound remains off by default.

Balance, collision geometry, damage, weapon choices, missiles, timers and challenge rules remain at the tested 1.4.0 behavior. Launch Runner, Trivia, public routes, the approved three-game cover and the separate Community service are unchanged. Local visual/input QA and installer verification passed, followed by actual public visual, sharing and file-identity checks.
