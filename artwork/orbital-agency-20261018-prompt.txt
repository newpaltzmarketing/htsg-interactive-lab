Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: finished square 2048 x 2048 background artwork for Agency Invaders, a humorous top-down arcade shooter about digital agency life. This is a BACKGROUND ONLY, not a mockup.
Primary request: an orbital creative agency headquarters, bringing office energy into space. A beautifully art-directed cinematic stylized 3D digital illustration with readable, witty office forms, warm amber accents and deep ink blue. It should feel like flying through the late-night agency, in orbit.
Scene: a spacious dark orbital atrium open to space. In the upper-middle distance, a crescent-shaped glass office station with clearly visible desk clusters, swivel chairs, desktop monitors and a conference room; frosted project boards with big blank colored sticky-note shapes. Behind it, a softly lit planet with a ring made from orderly layers of paper and folders, a subtle visual joke about endless briefs. A coffee mug-shaped distant satellite and a few very small paper silhouettes can occupy the far sides. No people or characters.
Composition is essential: square image designed for BOTH centered portrait crop and landscape crop. The key office station, desks and paper-ring planet MUST remain inside the central 42% of image width and 25–45% of image height, so the office theme survives narrow phone crops. Peripheral architecture can extend outward but cannot be the only agency clue. The middle and lower gameplay lane must stay open, very dark, low-contrast and calm. Avoid high-contrast objects, bright dots, particles or tiny clutter in the center flight lane. Background objects are distant, not foreground obstacles. Gently blurred depth toward the horizon, high-quality shapes and materials, no busy grain.
Lighting: restrained amber windows, diffuse cool rim light, atmospheric depth. Office details are visible but subordinate to foreground game sprites that will be added by code. Colors: midnight navy/charcoal backdrop, warm cream paper, amber/orange accents. Sophisticated, playful, welcoming rather than militaristic.
Text: NONE. No words, logos, labels, numbers, letter shapes, watermark, interface, borders, ship, enemies, bullets or UI. Not generic galaxy wallpaper: the office station and paperwork-ring planet are unmistakable. Full-bleed square artwork.
